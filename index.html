ABOUT BREANNA
I'm a dental receptionist, which means I spend my days answering phones, booking appointments, moving appointments, confirming appointments, rescheduling appointments and politely explaining that yes... you really do need that appointment. 😂

I'm basically the person standing between you and the dentist's waiting room. I know everyone's name, everyone's appointment time and, somehow, everyone's excuse for cancelling at the last minute.

I'm looking for someone funny, kind, confident, affectionate and capable of making a conversation more interesting than "hey."

Bonus points if you actually show up when you say you will.

Extra bonus points if you don't make me call you three times to remind you about your appointment.

And if your breath could knock over a houseplant, don't worry. I know exactly who to call. 😏🦷
