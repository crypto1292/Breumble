About Breanna
I'm a dental hygienist, which means I spend most of my professional life politely telling people that yes... their gums really do bleed when they floss. 😂

I know more about plaque, tartar, gingivitis, periodontal pockets and toothbrush technique than anyone probably needs to know on a date.

My patients come in nervous and leave with a cleaner smile and usually a slightly stronger commitment to flossing.

So naturally, when it comes to dating, I have developed a very specific appreciation for a great smile.

I'm not saying I'm going to inspect your molars before dinner...
But I'm also not saying I won't notice. 😏

Looking for someone funny, confident, kind, affectionate and capable of carrying a conversation without staring at their phone every twelve seconds.

Bonus points if you actually floss.
Double bonus points if you don't need me to explain why flossing isn't optional.

I love good conversation, spontaneous plans, laughing until my stomach hurts, movies, coffee, and people who don't take themselves too seriously.

My ideal date? Somewhere with great food, good conversation, zero awkward small talk and absolutely no discussion about someone's dental insurance.
