About Breanna
I'm a dental hygienist, which means I spend most of my professional life politely explaining that yes... your gums really do need attention.

I know more about plaque, tartar, gingivitis, periodontal pockets and proper brushing technique than the average person probably wants to hear about on a first date.

My patients come in nervous and leave with cleaner smiles and hopefully a renewed commitment to flossing.

So naturally I've developed a very specific appreciation for a great smile.

I'm not saying I'm going to inspect your molars before dinner...

But I'm also not saying I won't notice. 😏

I'm looking for someone funny, confident, kind, affectionate and capable of carrying a conversation without checking their phone every twelve seconds.

I love coffee, movies, good food, spontaneous plans, laughing until my stomach hurts and people who don't take themselves too seriously.

Bonus points if you actually floss.
Double bonus points if I don't have to remind you that flossing is not optional.
